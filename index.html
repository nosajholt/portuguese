Phrases for real life

Built from the 200 words, grouped by the situations that fill most days in Portugal. Use Slow first, then shadow at normal speed. In the sound guide, CAPITALS mark the stressed syllable and ' marks a vowel you barely say. Phrases and guides follow how people talk in Porto and the Minho. The iPhone voice speaks standard Lisbon Portuguese, so follow the guide for the Northern sounds.

How Portugal sounds

European Portuguese swallows unstressed vowels and turns many S's into "sh". This is the biggest difference from Brazilian, so learn the first ten rules first. Rules 11 to 14 are what make Porto and the Minho sound different from Lisbon.

8-week fast start

About 25 new words a week, 20 to 30 minutes a day. By week 8 you can handle cafés, shops, trains and basic paperwork, and you're on the road to the A2 (CIPLE) level.

Every day

  1. 5 min: replay this week's Sounds rule.
  2. 10 min: Words in Quiz mode. Say it before you tap.
  3. 10 min: Phrases. Play, pause, repeat aloud (shadowing).
  4. 1 min out loud: describe your day using 3 new words.

After week 8

  • Grow to 1,000 words. That gets you to about 80% of everyday conversation.
  • Use resources made for Portugal, not Brazil: RTP Play (free Portuguese TV), Practice Portuguese, and Portuguese podcasts.
